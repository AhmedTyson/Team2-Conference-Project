Knowledge Base & Support

How can we assist you?

Find instant answers regarding bookings, AI itinerary creation, membership tiers, and partner agency coordination.

How does Itinera verify hotels and experiences?
Every property listed on Itinera is vetted through our physical inspection guidelines or verified agency partners. We cross-reference traveler reviews, room standards, culinary hygiene, and service credentials.
What are the cancellation and refund terms for guided tours?
Cancellations made at least 48 hours prior to the scheduled departure are entitled to a full refund or voucher credit. For bespoke agency itineraries, custom terms may apply as itemized in your checkout receipt.
How does the AI Concierge work?
Our AI Concierge leverages real catalog availability, local weather forecasts, and travel style preferences to synthesize day-by-day schedules complete with dining reservations and route maps.
Can travel agencies register and manage client itineraries?
Yes! Certified agencies can access our dedicated Agency Portal to receive customer trip requests, design custom proposals, and generate branded PDF vouchers.
