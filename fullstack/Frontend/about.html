Our Mission

We make travel seamless, personal, and unforgettable. From the bustling streets of New York to the sun-drenched islands of Greece, Itinera connects you with authentic stays and curated experiences.

Our story

Born from a student team project at the Conference Project, Itinera grew into a full travel platform — planning, booking, weather, and reviews in one place.

What we value

Authenticity, transparency, and human support. Every journey we craft is built on those three pillars.
